# Burj Khalifa — structural research brief (sourced)
Sources: [A1] Abdelrazaq SMAR 2011 https://plus.empa.ch/smar-conferences-data/SMAR_2011_Proceedings/papers/KN%20AB.pdf · [A2] Abdelrazaq Design & Construction Planning https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0551/4300/6301/files/BURJ-1.pdf · [BKN] Baker/Korista/Novak (SOM) text via https://www.aboutcivil.org/burj-khalifa-design-construction-structural-details.html · SOM https://www.som.com/projects/burj-khalifa/ · RWDI https://rwdi.com/en_ca/projects/burj-khalifa

1. Buttressed core: hexagonal central core + 3 wings; each wing buttresses the other two; corridor walls (650 mm) = webs, hammerhead walls + nose columns at wing ends = flanges carrying overturning moment [A1][BKN]. Core walls 1300→500 mm, link (coupling) beams at every level 800–1100 mm deep — ductile "fuse" elements [A1].
   Reinforced concrete from foundation to level 155/156; structural steel braced spire from L156 to ~750 m; pinnacle steel pipe Ø2100×60 → Ø1200×30 to 828 m [A1]. Unoccupied spire ≈ 244 m (CTBUH via BD+C).
   Foundation: 3.7 m pile-supported raft; ~194 piles Ø1.5 m, ~43–45 m long, 3000 t each [A1][BKN].
2. Outriggers: RC four-storey shear-wall panels at the mechanical levels link core to perimeter (nose) columns; 5 sets distributed up the height [A1][BKN]. Exact floors NOT sourced — don't show floor numbers.
3. Setbacks spiral upward; "at every seventh floor one outer bay peels away" [A1]; wind encounters a different shape at each tier so vortices can't organise [SOM][RWDI]. Count uncertain ("24 major" — Baker); say "spiral setbacks".
4. Dynamics [BKN]: mode 1 sway T = 11.3 s, mode 2 T = 10.2 s, torsion (mode 5) T = 4.3 s. Measured matched FE within 2–3 % incl. higher modes [A1]. Top sway in wind up to ~2 m (Baker via press, uncertain).
5. Seismic: UBC97 Zone 2a (Z = 0.15), site-specific response spectrum [BKN]. Seismic did NOT govern the RC tower (wind did); seismic DID govern the podium and the steel spire [BKN]. "Designed for magnitude 7" = press myth; magnitude isn't a design parameter (site ground motion is).
   Observed: 2008 Bandar Abbas quake → 2.76/3.82 milli-g at L139 [A1]. July 2010 S-Iran M5.8 (<1 milli-g at site) had frequency content matching the pinnacle → pinnacle in resonance [A1].
6. Beyond-design critical locations (ENGINEERING REASONING, not documented): (a) steel spire/pinnacle and its connection at L156 (concrete→steel stiffness jump; higher-mode whip) — first damage; (b) base: core, wing walls, nose columns, raft/piles — largest overturning moment & base shear; (c) outrigger levels / setback transitions (force discontinuities); (d) coupling beams yield first by design (intended, not collapse).
7. Short vs long period: T ≈ 0.1 s per storey. Low/mid-rise (0.1–1 s) resonate with high-frequency near-field shaking (Mexico City 1985: 6–15-storey hit hardest, lake-bed ~2 s). Tall long-period towers respond to long-period waves of distant big quakes (2011 Tohoku → Osaka high-rises T 6–7 s shaken by basin amplification).
Caption guidance: magnitude ≠ site shaking (depends on distance) — frame film as a hypothetical "near-field" simulation; M10 never recorded (max M9.5 Chile 1960) → tag "hypothetical / beyond design". Avoid: "designed for M7", outrigger floor numbers, "27 setbacks", "Burj evacuated 2013".
